HOW IT WORKS

Set it up once. Then it
sells while you work.

1

Tell it about your shop

Add your products, prices and opening hours. Set how low you’ll go on each item. Show it a few of your own replies so it writes like you.

2

It talks to your customers

It answers questions from your catalog, quotes prices, and negotiates in small steps, never below your lowest price. Replies come with a natural pause.

3

You step in when it matters

Orders, complaints and questions it can’t answer reach your WhatsApp. Reply yourself and the assistant steps back so you never talk over each other.

YOU HOLD THE PRICE

The assistant can’t
give away your margin.

For every item you decide whether to show the price, give a range, or keep it for yourself. If customers may bargain, you set the lowest price you’ll accept. That number is stored separately: the assistant never sees it, and prices come from a calculator, not from the AI’s imagination.

  • Five ways to talk price: show it, “starts from”, a range, only after questions, or “I quote myself”.
  • Discounts with conditions: only when they buy two, pay an advance, or are returning customers.
  • Below your lowest price? It brings you in instead of agreeing.

QUESTIONS

Things shop owners ask us

Will it give discounts I didn’t allow?

No. The illustrative pricing engine follows the lowest-price and discount rules you set.

What if it doesn’t know the answer?

It should ask you to take over, instead of inventing stock, price, or product details.

Do I need a new phone number?

ChatDeal is designed to work with your business WhatsApp number. Eligibility and onboarding depend on WhatsApp platform requirements.

Can I still reply myself?

Yes. You stay in control and can pause the assistant when you want to handle a conversation.

What does it cost?

See the pricing cards above for the proposed Starter, Growth and Scale plans. The final offer will be confirmed at launch.

Is my customers’ data safe?

Customer privacy and access controls are priorities. Full security and privacy documentation will be available before launch.
